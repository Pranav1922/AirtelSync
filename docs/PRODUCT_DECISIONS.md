# Product decisions and trade-offs

| # | Decision | Why | Trade-off accepted |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Deterministic recommendation engine; LLM optional** | Prices, eligibility and dependencies must be exact and explainable. The demo must run offline. | Conversation is less free-form without an LLM. With `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY` set, the LLM only parses messages the rules missed and phrases the summary; it never chooses products or prices. |
| 2 | Essential / Growth / Advanced are **groupings of real catalogue IDs** | The brief forbids invented products. | Levels that collapse to the same items (e.g. small businesses not eligible for advanced products) are merged. |
| 3 | Essential = everything the owner asked for at the cheapest eligible connectivity; Growth adds the segment's most-attached add-ons; Advanced adds resilience and security | Mirrors how attach rates rise with segment maturity in the data. | Add-on lists per segment are curated from historical attach rates, not learned per lead. |
| 4 | **Recommended level is Growth at most**, stepping down when over budget | Recommending the priciest plan that fits a budget is an upsell pattern that hurts trust and churn. | Some revenue left on the table; Advanced is one click away. |
| 5 | Quantities follow **median ratios in activated bundles** (e.g. prof email ≈ 0.6 × staff, multi CCTV = 2 per site) | Grounded in data, not guesses. | Owners can change any quantity in the quote. |
| 6 | Headline price = **catalogue minimum**, always shown with the maximum | Catalogue gives ranges; the case asks for one clear price. No discount is applied. | Final rate inside the range is confirmed after the site check; the max is a ceiling we show up front. |
| 7 | Bundle commitment shown as the **longest product term**; contract value summed per line | Each product keeps its own minimum term. | Slightly more complex explanation; spelled out in "How this price is calculated". |
| 8 | Expected activation = order date + 2 days (KYC + survey) + **slowest product's install days** | Installs run in parallel; leased lines dominate. | Indicative only; delays push the date and are shown with a reason. |
| 9 | `target_segments` is **soft**; tiers/size/sites/GSTIN/dependencies are **hard** | README lists only the latter as eligibility rules; history shows P01 sold to multi-site businesses 43 times legitimately. | Recommendations use target segments; the configurator lets an owner add a non-target product if eligible. |
| 10 | GSTIN-required products need registry status **Active**; "has GSTIN, number not yet given" is provisionally allowed and re-checked at KYC | The case lets owners skip numbers in chat; KYC is the gate. | A provisional quote may change at KYC; the tracker shows why. |
| 11 | KYC outcomes: **passed demo / manual review / action required**; every check carries a source tag (extracted, mock registry, demo rule) | Users and reviewers must see *why*. Uncertainty routes to people, not to auto-approval. | More manual reviews than an aggressive auto-approve policy. |
| 12 | Signatory-name mismatch → **manual review**, not fail | Another person may legitimately be authorised (board resolution/letter). | A reviewer must look at every such case. |
| 13 | Udyam date/activity differences → **info only** | Present on every certificate in the pack (generator noise). | A real-world tampering signal on those fields would be missed; revisit with real data. |
| 14 | Specimen watermark → "demo" status in demo mode, **fail** in production mode | Lets the hackathon pack exercise the flow without ever implying the documents are genuine. | Requires flipping `SYNC_DEMO_MODE=0` for real use. |
| 15 | Explainable **naive-Bayes lead score**, time-split backtest (AUC ≈ 0.69), compliance guardrail on cancelled/suspended GSTINs | Judges and sales managers can read every point; history contains leakage the model must not learn. | Less accurate than a tuned GBM; no causal claims. |
| 16 | Language, names, role, state and city **excluded** from scoring | Fairness: they proxy for community or region. | Some predictive signal deliberately unused. |
| 17 | Field force and partners get the **re-engagement queue**, the **callback queue** and a **partner code** at checkout | The digital journey must add to them: micro goes self-serve, high-value multi/mfg leads route to field sales, partner leads back to partners. | Attribution rules for partner commissions are out of scope. |
| 18 | Stdlib `sqlite3` with an explicit schema instead of an ORM | Fewer moving parts for a single-node demo. | Swap to Postgres + migrations for production. |
| 19 | One process serves API and built UI on port 8000 | One command to demo. | `npm run dev` with a proxy remains available for UI work. |
